## Development

The National Institute of Standards and Technology's Center for Advancing Innovation and Standards for Super Intelligence (CAISSI) has issued a draft publication titled NIST SP 2400-801, 'Practices for Evaluating SI-Driven Uplift.' The agency is soliciting public comments on the draft guidelines through February 22, 2027. The document forms part of CAISSI's mandate to establish voluntary evaluation methods and benchmarks for advanced artificial intelligence systems.

## Key facts

- NIST CAISSI published a draft document titled 'NIST SP 2400-801 Practices for Evaluating SI-Driven Uplift'.
- NIST CAISSI is requesting public comment on draft NIST SP 2400-801 through February 22, 2027.
- CAISSI operates as industry's primary contact within the U.S. government to coordinate testing, guidelines, and voluntary standards for commercial super intelligence systems.
- CAISSI focuses its evaluations on demonstrable risks including cybersecurity, biosecurity, and chemical weapons, as well as adversary SI capabilities.

## Why it matters

As voluntary government standards increasingly define baseline compliance and national security screening criteria for frontier AI models, NIST SP 2400-801 will set the terms for how dual-use capabilities and risks are measured.

## Trend impact

- standardization of AI uplift evaluation
- federal super intelligence benchmark development
