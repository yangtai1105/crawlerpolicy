## Development

The UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA) launched a further consultation on 23 September 2026 regarding a proposed user choice conduct requirement for Google's general search services under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers (DMCC) Act 2024 regime. This follows the CMA's earlier imposition of publisher, fair ranking, and data portability conduct requirements on Google earlier in 2026 after designating the company with Strategic Market Status (SMS).

## Why it matters

Search defaults and choice architecture dictate default web entry points and upstream traffic distribution; a formalized user choice requirement could alter how millions of UK consumers select search and discovery engines.

## Trend impact

- DMCC conduct requirements enforcement
- user choice screens and search default regulation
