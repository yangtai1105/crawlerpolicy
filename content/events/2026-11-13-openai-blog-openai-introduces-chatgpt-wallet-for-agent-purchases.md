## Development

OpenAI announced ChatGPT Wallet, providing a mechanism for AI agents to complete purchases. The capability is deployed across ChatGPT Work, Codex, and dots. Transactions operate with user approval and use existing card protections.

## Key facts

- OpenAI introduced ChatGPT Wallet as a secure payment mechanism for AI agents.
- The wallet functions across agents in ChatGPT Work, Codex, and dots.
- Purchases executed via ChatGPT Wallet require user approval.
- Transactions rely on existing card protections.

## Why it matters

Enabling AI agents to initiate and finalize commerce directly connects automated task planning with economic execution, shifting agent utility from recommendations to closed-loop transactions.
