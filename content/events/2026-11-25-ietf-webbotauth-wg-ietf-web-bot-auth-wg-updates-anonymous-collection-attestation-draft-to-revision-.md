## Development

The IETF Web Bot Auth Working Group published revision -03 of the Internet-Draft titled 'Attested Conduct for Anonymous Web Data Collection: Use Cases, Requirements, and a Tiered Model'. The document expanded by two pages to 16 pages and now reflects three associated IPR disclosures, up from two. The draft outlines requirements and tiered behavioral frameworks for automated crawlers seeking access without disclosing operator identity.

## Key facts

- The Internet-Draft 'draft-mckenna-webbotauth-collection-attestation-03' was published on 2026-11-24.
- The document length increased from 14 pages in revision -02 to 16 pages in revision -03.
- The number of associated IPR disclosures listed for the draft increased from 2 to 3.
- The draft is titled 'Attested Conduct for Anonymous Web Data Collection: Use Cases, Requirements, and a Tiered Model'.

## Why it matters

Balancing automated data collection with site protection often forces an unpalatable choice between invasive crawler tracking and blunt IP blocking; standardized attestation tiers offer a structured path for compliant anonymous collection.

## Trend impact

- standardized anonymous crawler attestation
- intellectual property disclosures in bot governance
