### AI Agent Infrastructure Updates (August 16 - August 30, 2026)

1.  **Google Agent Development Kit Introduces Cryptographic Identity for Agent Actions**
    Google's Agent Development Kit (ADK) now supports cryptographic identity and non-repudiation for agent actions. This feature ensures that every write operation performed by an AI agent can be cryptographically signed, providing verifiable proof of the agent's identity and preventing denial of actions. This is a crucial development for building zero-trust AI agents and enhancing accountability in multi-agent architectures.
