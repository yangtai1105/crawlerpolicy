2.4 2.4 2.3 2.2 2.1 2.0 1.4 1.3 1.2 1.1 1.0 C2PA Specifications The Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) addresses the prevalence of misleading information online through the development of technical standards for certifying the source and history (or provenance) of media content. C2PA is a Joint Development Foundation project. This site contains the various specifications and informative documents produced by the C2PA.
