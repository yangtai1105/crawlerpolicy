## News

[DataDome published a 10-question buyer's guide for evaluating bot and agent trust management providers](https://datadome.co/learning-center/top-10-questions-to-ask-a-bot-management-provider/). The framework covers SaaS delivery model, real-time edge protection with 30+ PoPs, false positive rates (benchmarking 0.01% as ideal vs. 0.75% from competitors), deployment flexibility across CDNs and integrations, UI/dashboard navigation, reporting capabilities, human UX preservation, dual server-side and client-side signal collection, AI/ML model maintenance with threat research oversight, and 24/7 threat research team monitoring. The guide positions these criteria as essential for distinguishing providers in a crowded market and explicitly [names DataDome as a Forrester Wave™ Leader in Bot Management Q3 2024](https://datadome.co/resources/forrester-wave-bot-management/).

## Why it matters

This vendor evaluation framework formalizes buying criteria that reflect DataDome's evolving market position and strategic messaging around intent-based detection and AI agent threat management. The emphasis on intent-based detection, real-time AI agent classification (vs. legacy WAF approaches), and threat research team oversight reinforces the pattern from recent DataDome posts—three items (April 22, April 16, April 7) highlight AI agent adoption as a market driver and position DataDome's intent-based detection as a differentiator. The false positive rate benchmark (0.01% vs. 0.75% from unnamed competitors) serves as implicit competitive positioning. For publishers and platform operators evaluating vendors, this checklist reflects enterprise expectations: SaaS-first delivery, global PoPs, multi-signal detection, and continuous threat monitoring. The guide does not announce new product capability but rather codifies evaluation standards that align with DataDome's existing messaging, suggesting consolidation of vendor positioning rather than ecosystem reshaping.
