## News

[DataDome announced Priority Protect](https://datadome.co/products/priority-protect/), a virtual waiting room solution that integrates real-time bot and AI agent detection with queue management. The product combines continuous in-session validation, AI-powered request classification on server and client side, customizable priority lanes, and intent-aware policies that differentiate between humans, trusted agents, and malicious bots. DataDome cited a real-world case where 31% of queue traffic (2.4 million of 7.8 million requests) during a midnight ticket sale was bot traffic, and initial customers report cleaner queues, reduced inventory losses, and faster wait times for legitimate users.

## Why it matters

This launch reflects [DataDome's ongoing pivot toward agentic commerce defense](https://datadome.co/threat-research/) as a core infrastructure capability, extending its bot detection expertise into queue management at a moment when AI agents operate 24/7 and turn every product drop or ticket release into a flash sale. Priority Protect is the fourth major DataDome product announcement in May 2026 (following the Agent Trust control plane, virtual waiting room guide, and threat analysis), signaling acceleration of the vendor's portfolio maturation in response to documented mainstream AI agent adoption. The product's emphasis on continuous re-validation throughout a session—rather than single-entry-point decisions—directly addresses the shape-shifting threat profile that traditional queue solutions cannot detect. For e-commerce and ticketing platforms, this represents a shift from treating waiting rooms as pure scalability infrastructure to treating them as a trust enforcement layer, implying that queue fairness and inventory protection will increasingly become competitive differentiators.
