## Summary

Cloudflare has introduced an Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) dashboard aimed at providing publishers with insights into AI bot traffic and downstream model visibility. Priced at $20 per month, the product monitors AI bot access, citation rates, and mention frequency across LLMs. The launch was reported by [Search Engine Land](https://searchengineland.com/cloudflare-upend-ai-visibility-space-498710).

## Key facts

- Cloudflare shipped a new AEO dashboard.
- The dashboard tracks AI bot access, citation rates, and mention rates by LLM.
- The tool provides publishers with data on AI traffic and visibility for $20/month.

## Insight

By uniting edge-level crawler log telemetry with LLM citation and mention tracking into a low-cost subscription, infrastructure providers are expanding directly into search and discovery analytics.

## Implication

Publishers gain an accessible baseline to evaluate whether allowing AI scrapers actually yields generative citations, while specialized SEO and AI visibility measurement vendors will face competitive pressure from edge platforms.

## Why it matters

Determining whether to block or allow AI crawlers has largely been an asymmetric gamble for website owners; pairing bot crawl telemetry directly with citation rates helps quantify the return on automated access.
