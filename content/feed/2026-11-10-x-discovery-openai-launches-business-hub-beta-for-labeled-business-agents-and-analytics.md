## Summary

OpenAI launched Business Hub in beta for eligible U.S. ecommerce and service businesses to claim brand profiles. The platform enables companies to deploy labeled Business Agents that answer customer queries using provided business data. Additionally, businesses can access aggregated reporting on mentions, clicks, and topics without OpenAI sharing underlying user conversations.

## Key facts

- OpenAI shipped Business Hub in beta.
- Access is restricted to eligible U.S. ecommerce and service businesses.
- Businesses can claim profiles and publish labeled Business Agents.
- Business Agents answer customer questions using provided business data.
- The hub provides aggregated insights covering mentions, clicks, and topics.
- Aggregated insights are provided without sharing user conversations.

## Insight

By introducing publisher-managed profiles and designated Business Agents alongside privacy-preserving discovery metrics, OpenAI is constructing a formal publisher-facing commercial layer within conversational search.

## Implication

Eligible U.S. merchants can directly supply data to manage brand presence inside ChatGPT, while digital marketing and SEO teams must adapt to closed-platform aggregated metrics rather than direct log or conversation visibility.

## Why it matters

As generative AI interfaces increasingly mediate product discovery and customer inquiries, structured self-serve portals like Business Hub dictate how commercial entities shape and measure their synthetic platform presence.
