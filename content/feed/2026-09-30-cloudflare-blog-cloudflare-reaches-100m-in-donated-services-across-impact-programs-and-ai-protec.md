## Summary

Cloudflare announced that its Impact programs, including Project Galileo and the Athenian Project, have surpassed $100 million in donated cybersecurity and infrastructure services. The company highlighted its ongoing expansion into assisting newsrooms and civil society against unauthorized AI scraping through donated Bot Management and AI Crawl control features. The initiative also details developer platform hackathons aimed at helping journalists navigate an AI-driven, post-search media environment.

## Insight

Infrastructure providers are bundling AI crawler governance and automated scraping defenses into non-profit and public-interest subsidy packages, establishing edge-level bot controls as standard defense mechanisms alongside conventional DDoS mitigation.

## Implication

Independent publishers, human rights organizations, and local news outlets enrolled in Project Galileo gain access to commercial-grade bot mitigation and AI crawler blocking without dedicated infrastructure budgets.

## Why it matters

As AI scrapers increase crawling volume across the open web, public-interest publishers face disproportionate resource constraints; subsidized edge controls offer smaller entities a practical mechanism to enforce machine access boundaries.
