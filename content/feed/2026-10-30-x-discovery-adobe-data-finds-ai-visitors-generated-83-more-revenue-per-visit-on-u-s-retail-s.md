## Summary

Adobe released benchmark data indicating that AI-driven visitors produced 83% more revenue per visit on U.S. retail websites in September 2026. This metric marks a significant surge from parity with standard traffic observed the previous year. The findings were accompanied by holiday shopping season projections for AI-referred retail activity.

## Key facts

- Adobe released data evaluating the economic output of AI visitors on U.S. retail sites.
- AI visitors generated 83% more revenue per visit on U.S. retail sites in September 2026.
- Revenue per visit from AI visitors was at parity with average visitors during the prior year.
- The data release included forecasts covering the upcoming holiday retail season.

## Insight

AI referral traffic is shifting from exploratory query volume to high-intent transactional conversion, demonstrating a measurable commercial premium over conventional web visits.

## Implication

Retailers and e-commerce platforms may re-evaluate automated access controls and bot mitigation policies, distinguishing high-intent AI traffic from non-converting crawlers.

## Why it matters

Demonstrating that AI traffic delivers substantially higher per-visit revenue challenges publisher assumptions that AI search intermediaries solely cannibalize monetization without delivering high-value downstream transactions.
