## Summary

Search Engine Land released an industry guide cataloging known content licensing agreements between major AI providers and publishers. The mapping covers partnerships involving the technology behind ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Meta AI. It evaluates the impact of commercial deals on organic search engine optimization and visibility across AI search interfaces.

## Key facts

- Search Engine Land published a comprehensive map of all known licensing deals between AI companies and publishers.
- The guide covers AI platforms and models powering ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, and Meta AI.
- The analysis investigates the implications of these commercial partnerships for SEO and AI search visibility.

## Insight

Licensing deals are increasingly analyzed not just as data acquisition agreements, but as structural factors influencing publisher visibility and retrieval priority within emerging AI search engines.

## Implication

Publishers weighing whether to license content or block automated scrapers may need to account for downstream AI search visibility alongside direct licensing revenue.

## Why it matters

As AI-driven search models replace traditional referral mechanisms, commercial licensing agreements may dictate which publishers maintain discovery and traffic in generative answer engines.
