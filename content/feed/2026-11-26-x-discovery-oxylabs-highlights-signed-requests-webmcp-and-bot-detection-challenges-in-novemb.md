Industry focus is shifting toward cryptographic identity and structured agent protocols like WebMCP as traditional heuristic bot detection scores struggle to reliably classify AI-driven web traffic.

## Implication

Scraping operators, webmasters, and anti-bot vendors will need to evaluate structured identity signals and signed request frameworks rather than relying solely on legacy fingerprinting and reputation scoring.

## Why it matters

As automated agents proliferate, legacy bot detection mechanisms increasingly risk misclassifying legitimate AI traffic, accelerating the adoption of formal agent identity standards.
