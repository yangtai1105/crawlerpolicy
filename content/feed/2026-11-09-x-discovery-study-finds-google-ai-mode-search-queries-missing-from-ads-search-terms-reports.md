## Summary

An analysis of more than 400 Google Ads accounts revealed that Google's claim of AI Mode queries being three times longer is not reflected in advertiser search terms reports. The analysis identified three possible explanations for why this AI search activity remains invisible to advertisers. The findings raise questions about reporting transparency and query visibility in Google's generative search interface.

## Key facts

- A study analyzed more than 400 Google Ads accounts.
- Google claimed that AI Mode queries run 3x longer than standard queries.
- The analysis found that the 3x longer query pattern is not reflected in Google Ads search terms reports.
- The report identified three possible explanations for why AI search activity is invisible in the reports.

## Insight

Advertiser-facing reporting mechanisms may systematically obscure or aggregate AI-driven conversational search interactions, creating an informational gap between public platform metrics and actionable campaign data.

## Implication

Search advertisers and digital marketers may be unable to evaluate or optimize for emerging AI query patterns directly through standard Google Ads reporting until visibility mechanisms are updated.

## Why it matters

If AI-assisted searches reshape user intent and query volume without surfacing in performance logs, publishers and advertisers lose the data needed to evaluate traffic attribution and ad spend effectiveness.
