## Summary

DataDome announced that its Agent Trust technology was named to Fast Company's 2026 Next Big Things in Tech list. The platform evaluates autonomous AI agents by assessing dynamic behavioral intent rather than static identity declarations alone. The system assigns AI agents a continuously updated 100-point trust score informed by behavioral telemetry.

## Insight

Anti-bot and web traffic management systems are shifting from static identity checks (such as verified user-agents or reverse DNS) to real-time intent analysis, assigning dynamic trust scores across an agent's entire session.

## Implication

Autonomous agent operators and scraper developers will face granular, ongoing behavioral scrutiny where individual session actions, rather than verified credentials alone, determine access permissions.

## Why it matters

As automated agents proliferate, perimeter defense is migrating toward behavioral scoring, making declarative crawler controls insufficient on their own to guarantee site access.
