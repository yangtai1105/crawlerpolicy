## Summary

Travel and experiences booking platform Klook has renewed its bot mitigation partnership with DataDome to defend high-demand ticket sales and API traffic. The platform combines DataDome Bot Protect with internal fraud controls to counter automated threats while monitoring traffic from emerging AI-powered browser tools. Klook stated that its strategy is to apply risk-based controls rather than blocking all automated interactions by default.

## Key facts

- Klook renewed its bot mitigation partnership with DataDome to protect web and API traffic, specifically during high-demand flash sales and ticket events.
- DataDome Bot Protect's AI detection models work alongside Klook's internal Payments & Fraud team, security infrastructure, and business-specific risk controls.
- Klook highlighted traffic from AI-powered browser tools and agents as an emerging operational challenge.
- The Klook Payments & Fraud team stated: "The key challenge is to distinguish legitimate automation from malicious activity without unnecessarily restricting genuine users or legitimate AI use cases."
- Klook's security stance is to "Avoid blocking all automation by default" and use a risk-based approach to allow legitimate automated task interactions.
- During major sales events, DataDome's SOC and Galileo Threat Research teams collaborate with Klook to investigate attack patterns and review false positives.

## Insight

Ticketing and travel platforms are adjusting bot mitigation models to accommodate consumer-delegated AI agents, moving away from binary bot blocking toward risk-weighted discrimination between abusive scrapers and legitimate automated purchasing tools.

## Implication

Autonomous booking tools and AI browser agents will increasingly have to navigate behavioral anti-bot evaluation and API-layer mitigations during peak retail events.

## Why it matters

As user bookings transition from manual browsing to AI-assisted workflows, platforms must develop mechanisms that allow legitimate autonomous agents through without reopening doors to scalper bots.
