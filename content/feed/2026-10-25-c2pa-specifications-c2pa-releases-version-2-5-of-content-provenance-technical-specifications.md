## Summary

The Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) has added version 2.5 to its official specification library at spec.c2pa.org. The update follows version 2.4 as the latest release in the organization's open standards suite for certifying media origin and history.

## Key facts

- The Coalition for Content Provenance and Authenticity (C2PA) added version 2.5 to its technical specification listings at spec.c2pa.org.
- Version 2.5 is listed directly above version 2.4, marking the newest release in the 2.x specification lineage.
- The specification suite covers Content Credentials, Content Credentials JSON (crJSON), Attestations, and the Soft Binding API.
- C2PA operates as a Joint Development Foundation project addressing misleading online information through provenance and source certification standards.

## Insight

C2PA maintains an iterative cadence for its provenance architecture, moving from version 2.4 to 2.5 to refine technical standards spanning Content Credentials, crJSON, attestations, and AI/ML guidance.

## Implication

Implementers of C2PA standards, including content creators, distribution platforms, and verification tools, will need to review version 2.5 for updated schemas, attestations, and binding requirements.

## Why it matters

As automated crawlers and AI platforms face increasing pressure to verify content authenticity and track provenance, updates to C2PA standards establish the technical baseline for digital content validation across the web.
