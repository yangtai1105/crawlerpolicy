## Summary

HUMAN Security published its October 2026 agentic traffic benchmark, reporting an 8x surge in AI agent web requests since August 2026. Meta and OpenAI now account for approximately 90% of all agentic web traffic, led by Meta's Muse capturing 59% of the overall market. The data highlights a rapid migration toward platform-integrated assistants, while standalone agentic browsers like Perplexity's Comet lose significant traffic share.

## Key facts

- Agentic web traffic grew 2.6x (157.5%) month-over-month in October 2026 and roughly 8x (697.5%) since August 2026.
- Meta and OpenAI collectively generate approximately 90% of total agentic web requests.
- Meta's Muse captured 59% of all agentic traffic in October 2026, quadrupling its volume month-over-month and generating 3.7 times more requests than ChatGPT Agent.
- OpenAI captured roughly 30% of traffic across ChatGPT Agent (15.9%), Codex Browser (8.8%), and the Codex Chrome extension (5.2%).
- Perplexity's Comet browser saw its traffic share fall from 41% in August to 3.6% in October, representing the only top-10 agent to shrink in absolute request volume.
- E-commerce was the primary destination sector at 59.88% of agent traffic, followed by travel at 19.3% and media at 19.1%.
- Product and search pages accounted for 85% of all agentic requests, while checkout and payment represented 1.9% of traffic despite a 117% month-over-month absolute increase.
- The benchmark measures AI agent traffic via dedicated technical signals rather than crawler traffic, noting crawlers are an unreliable proxy for agentic usage.

## Insight

Agent traffic is rapidly centralizing around established mega-platforms embedding agents into existing user surfaces rather than standalone agentic browsers, with research and search page interactions dominating over transactional checkout execution.

## Implication

Websites and e-commerce operators face an abrupt transition from managing traditional search and training crawlers to handling high-frequency automated browsing agents controlled by a duopoly of Meta and OpenAI.

## Why it matters

Understanding the separation between passive crawler traffic and active delegated agent browsing is critical for web defense and authorization architectures as agent-driven visits scale exponentially.
