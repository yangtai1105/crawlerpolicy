## Summary

Perplexity AI has shipped Multiagent, a new effort level for complex queries. The system uses a lead agent to break down tasks and delegate work to specialized agents powered by distinct frontier models. These sub-agents share intermediate findings back to the lead agent, which synthesizes the final output.

## Key facts

- Perplexity AI shipped Multiagent as a new effort level.
- A lead agent decomposes tasks and delegates to a team of sub-agents.
- The delegated agents are powered by different frontier models.
- The sub-agents share findings before the lead agent synthesizes a final answer.

## Insight

Agentic search systems are transitioning from single-model sequential pipelines to multi-agent architectures that orchestrate multiple frontier models simultaneously to handle distinct sub-tasks.

## Implication

Web hosts and content providers may see compounded autonomous query and retrieval patterns per single user prompt as multi-agent frameworks distribute parallel exploration across sources.

## Why it matters

The deployment of coordinated sub-agents across heterogeneous models amplifies automated research complexity and machine access volume for end-user discovery tools.
