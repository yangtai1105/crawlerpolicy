Stratechery published an interview with marketing analyst Eric Seufert examining commercial models for agentic commerce. As agent-driven purchasing emerges, the debate over monetization is dividing between traditional advertising adapted for algorithmic buyers and purpose-built retail interfaces designed specifically for autonomous agents.

Retailers and digital advertising platforms will need to determine whether to invest in algorithmic advertising protocols or rebuild storefront APIs to cater to delegated consumer agents.

If autonomous agents replace manual human browsing, web discovery and monetization models will shift from influencing human attention to negotiating with automated purchasing intermediaries.
