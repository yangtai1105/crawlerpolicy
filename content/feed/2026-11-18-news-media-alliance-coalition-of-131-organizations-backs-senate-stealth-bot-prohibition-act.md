## Summary

A coalition of 131 organizations representing news publishers, content industries, and technology groups sent a support letter to US Senators Roger Wicker and Ben Ray Luján. The letter commends the senators for introducing the Stealth Bot Prohibition Act in the Senate. The signatories represent dozens of US states and international jurisdictions.

## Key facts

- A group of 131 organizations delivered a letter supporting the introduction of the Stealth Bot Prohibition Act in the US Senate.
- The coalition delivered the letter to Senators Roger Wicker and Ben Ray Luján, thanking them for introducing the bill.
- Signatories include news publishers of all sizes, content industries, and technology organizations.
- The supporting organizations represent dozens of US states and international countries.

## Insight

Publishers and content creators are coordinating broad multi-industry legislative coalitions to push statutory bans against deceptive or unannounced automated scraping.

## Implication

If advanced, the legislation could create federal legal consequences or disclosure mandates for automated crawler operators that disguise identity or bypass publisher access controls.

## Why it matters

Broad industry backing spanning 131 organizations indicates concerted political pressure in Congress to legally restrict evasive crawling practices used to harvest content.
