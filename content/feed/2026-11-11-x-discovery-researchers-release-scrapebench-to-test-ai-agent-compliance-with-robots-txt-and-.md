## Summary

Researchers have released ScrapeBench, an evaluation benchmark designed to assess whether AI agents honor scraping prohibitions across 1,626 websites. The benchmark specifically tests compliance against both technical robots.txt instructions and website terms of service restrictions.

## Key facts

- Researchers introduced ScrapeBench, an empirical benchmark to test AI agent compliance with scraping prohibitions.
- The benchmark measures adherence to both robots.txt directives and website terms of service clauses.
- ScrapeBench evaluates agent behavior across an assessment set of 1,626 sites.

## Insight

Evaluating AI agents against both machine-readable protocols and legal terms marks a shift toward testing whether autonomous software respects site-level access boundaries beyond traditional search indexers.

## Implication

Agent frameworks and model developers will face standardized empirical scrutiny over whether their tools bypass explicit scraping restrictions when browsing the live web.

## Why it matters

As autonomous browsing agents proliferate, website owners need to know whether technical directives and legal terms are actually respected by machine operators.
